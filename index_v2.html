About Me

Real Business Problems

Technology

I'm Jamil Hosen, a serial entrepreneur and full-stack developer based in Bangladesh. I founded Blazet IT Agency with a vision to empower businesses through technology.


Working with daily operations changed my perspective. I saw firsthand the challenges sellers face - from manual payment tracking to slow payouts. That's why I'm building www.yourplatform.com \u2014 Bangladesh's first localized e-commerce store builder like Salla/Zid.


My expertise spans mobile development, web applications, cybersecurity, and AI-powered solutions. I believe in building products that solve real problems for real people.
